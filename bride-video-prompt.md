# Bride-side invitation video – generation prompts

Modelled on the Instagram reel https://www.instagram.com/p/DU0OUTdCExi/ (Epic Invites):
a 9:16 vertical video, about 60 seconds, one scene per event, each scene a cinematic
3D-rendered Indian wedding tableau with a decorative frame at the top carrying the event
name, date, time and venue. Text is filled from the Sablaniya family card.

Reel sequence (what it does, second by second):

| Time | Scene |
|---|---|
| 0–4 s | Top-down wooden table with marigolds, diyas, rose petals, gold mandala; gold calligraphy "Wedding Invitation" |
| 5–9 s | Red velvet envelope opens, cream card slides out |
| 10–13 s | Ganesha idol in a floral pavilion, Sanskrit shloka on a scroll above |
| 14–18 s | Ornate gold arch frame with the couple's names and parents' names |
| 19–27 s | Event 1 (Tilak): groom seated with family, pink frame with title/date/time/venue |
| 28–31 s | Event 2 (Haldi): family in yellow |
| 32–35 s | Event 3 (Mehandi): couple in green and orange |
| 36–43 s | Event 4 (Sangeet): couple dancing on a lit stage |
| 44–47 s | Event 5 (Baraat): groom in a flower-decked vintage car |
| 48–51 s | Event 6 (Wedding): couple at the mandap |
| 52–55 s | Event 7 (Reception): couple on a sofa |

---

## How to use

* Most AI video tools (Google Veo in Gemini, Runway, Kling, Pika, Hailuo, Luma) generate
  5–10 second clips, and none of them render long text reliably. Generate each scene
  **without text**, then add the title frames in CapCut, Canva or InShot and join the clips.
  A `[TEXT OVERLAY]` block below every scene gives you exactly what to type.
* If your tool accepts a reference image, upload `assets/photo-4.jpg` or `assets/photo-3.jpg`
  so the couple's faces, the maroon saree and the cream sherwani stay consistent.
* Paste the **Master style** block first (or into the "style" / "negative prompt" field),
  then one scene prompt at a time. Generate at 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps.
* When you have the finished MP4, send it here and it will be compressed and installed as
  the bride page intro, the same way the current envelope video was.

## Master style (prepend to every scene)

```
Vertical 9:16 cinematic 3D-rendered Indian wedding invitation video, Pixar-like realism, warm golden-hour lighting, shallow depth of field, slow dolly-in camera, soft bokeh, floating rose petals and marigold petals, gold sparkle particles, palette of deep maroon, ivory cream, antique gold and marigold orange, Rajasthani royal haveli setting with carved sandstone arches and jaali screens, peacock accents, hanging marigold garlands (genda phool), brass diyas. Bride: young Indian woman, long black hair, small bindi, deep maroon embroidered saree with gold border, silver necklace. Groom: young Indian man, short black hair, light beard, cream bandhgala sherwani with gold buttons and a pocket square. Keep both faces consistent across scenes. Leave the top 30 % of the frame visually calm for a title card. No text, no watermark, no logos, no extra fingers, no distorted faces.
```
